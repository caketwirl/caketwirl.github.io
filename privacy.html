Privacy Policy — Cake Twirl

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Cake Twirl ("the Game") is a mobile puzzle game made by Andrea Signorelli ("we", "us"), Matthews Road, Liberta, St. John's, Antigua and Barbuda. Questions about privacy: asignorelli1989@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • No account, no login, no sign-up. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or any other contact detail.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Coins, stars, levels, boosters, settings and gift cakes are saved on your device only. We have no servers and we do not receive your game data.
  • No analytics. The Game has no statistics or tracking tool of its own.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google receives some information from your device (section 3). Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details (section 4).
  • We do not sell your personal data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob) and your consent

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited: optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward, and, from level 20, a short ad after some won levels. There are no banner ads. If you buy No ads, the ads between levels stop; reward videos remain available if you want them.

To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the Game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (country or city, not your precise location — the Game never asks for location permission);
  • information about the device and the app: device model, operating system and version, language, app version;
  • interactions with ads: which ads were shown, taps, video views;
  • diagnostic information of the ad SDK, such as crash and performance data.

Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland). Before ads use your data, the Game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can accept personalised ads, refuse them, or choose by purpose. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use only the information needed to show the ad, count it and fight fraud. Other regions with privacy laws that give you a right to opt out (for example some US states) get the corresponding Google message where it applies.

Changing your choice. At any time: Settings › Ad privacy choices in the Game reopens the consent form (the entry appears where the law requires a consent choice). On your phone you can also reset or delete your advertising ID (Android Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on the phone), which stops the old ID from being used for personalised ads.

How Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google acts as an independent controller for the personalised ads it serves; its own terms and policies apply.

Legal bases (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies): your consent for personalised ads and for storing or reading information on your device for ads (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy rules); for non-personalised or limited ads, our legitimate interest in funding a free game with ads (Art. 6(1)(f)), within the limits of your consent choice for device storage.

4. Purchases

The Game sells optional items (coin packs, a starter pack, a continue pack and "No ads") through Google Play Billing. Payment happens entirely in Google Play: Google processes your payment details under the Google Play terms and Google's privacy policy, and we never receive your card number, name or billing address. The Game receives from Google Play only what it needs to give you the item (which product was bought and a confirmation of the purchase) and keeps it on your device. Google Play also provides us, as the developer, with order reports (for example product, price, country, order number) for accounting and refunds.

Purchases are verified through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which acts as our service provider: it receives the purchase receipt, a random anonymous app user ID created on your device, and basic technical data (app version, operating system, country, IP address) to confirm purchases and let you restore them. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Sharing a gift cake

When you finish a gift cake, the Game can create a picture of it (for example "Happy Birthday, MIA!" and "Made with Cake Twirl"). Only if you tap Share does the Game hand the picture to your phone's share sheet; you choose the app and the person, and that app's own terms apply. We do not receive the picture or the name. Names are checked against a list of offensive words before they can be used on a cake.

6. What we do not do

  • No account and no sign-in, no social login.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK of our own, no crash-reporting service of our own.
  • No location permission, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files access. The Game's Android permissions are internet access (ads, purchases), vibration (haptic feedback) and the advertising ID (ads).
  • No push notifications.
  • No servers of our own: we cannot see who plays or how.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising beyond what is described in section 3.

Google Play itself may collect information when you download and use apps (for example crash reports if you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google); that is covered by Google's privacy policy, not by us.

7. Children

Cake Twirl is made for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. We collect no personal data ourselves; if you believe a child has used the Game and want us to look at it, write to asignorelli1989@gmail.com.

8. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you reset your progress, clear the app's data or uninstall the Game.
  • Google (ads, Play Billing): according to Google's retention policies, described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention.
  • We keep e-mails you send us only as long as needed to answer and for our records, then delete them.

9. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the safeguards required by law for such transfers (for example the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier use), to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Because we hold no personal data about you on our side, most of these rights are exercised directly:

  • Game data: Settings › Reset progress, or uninstall the Game.
  • Ads: Settings › Ad privacy choices (consent), and your phone's ads settings (reset or delete the advertising ID). For data Google holds, use Google's tools at https://myaccount.google.com or contact Google.
  • Anything else: write to asignorelli1989@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If the Game starts handling data differently (for example a new feature, analytics or a cloud save), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released, and describe the change in the release notes.
